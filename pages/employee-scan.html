How it works
  1. Security displays a QR that refreshes every 2 minutes.
  2. Scan the current QR with your employee account.
  3. FastAPI validates the QR token.
  4. If valid, choose Check in or Check out.
  5. Attendance is submitted to the documented attendance endpoint.
An expired or invalid QR must be scanned again after security displays the refreshed code.
